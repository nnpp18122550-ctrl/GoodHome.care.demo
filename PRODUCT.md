# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JavaScript

## Users

Homeowners and residential property managers in Thailand who want a simple, trustworthy way to book routine home maintenance and cleaning services. The primary job is to compare services, understand pricing, and request help without navigating a complex booking flow.

## Product Purpose

GoodHome.care is a home-services landing page for a local provider that offers routine cleaning, air-conditioner care, and garden upkeep. The product exists to make those services easy to discover, evaluate, and request from a single trusted entry point.

## Positioning

The product positions itself as a convenient one-stop home care service, emphasizing convenience, cleanliness, and maintenance quality for residential spaces. It competes by combining a clear service menu, transparent pricing signals, and a fast inquiry path instead of a complex marketplace model.

## Operating Context

The site is a marketing and inquiry surface for home services rather than a full service-management platform. Visitors are likely evaluating a provider while browsing on desktop or mobile, then deciding whether to contact the business for booking. The content and imagery reflect a Thai residential context and the usual flow of a local service business: service discovery, price understanding, and direct inquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services currently advertised: home cleaning, air-conditioning cleaning, and garden/lawn care.
- The interface includes navigation, service cards, pricing sections, trust messaging, and a booking/contact inquiry path.
- Current implementation is a static front-end and does not include a live backend, account system, or booking database.
- Content language is Thai-first, with English labels used for category framing and headings.
- Future work must treat the current product as a residential home-care service business, not a general property management platform.

## Brand Commitments

The business name and brand expression are centered on home care, reliability, and a clean residential feel. The present world is calm, approachable, and practical rather than luxury or highly engineered. The brand currently signals clear, local service value over premium positioning.

## Product Principles

1. Make home maintenance feel simple and reassuring.
2. Prioritize clarity of service and pricing over complex flows.
3. Serve residential customers who want convenience and trust.
4. Keep the experience grounded in a local, practical service model.
5. Support decisions through visible benefits, proof points, and direct inquiry actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product should remain readable, mobile-friendly, and accessible to a broad residential audience. The current content is Thai-first and should avoid making accessibility or language support a secondary concern in future design work.
